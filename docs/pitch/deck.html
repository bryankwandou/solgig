SolGig

A marketplace where creators sell files and services for SOL, and where an AI agent can find, buy and receive a product through one endpoint without a person clicking anything.

solgig.vercel.app01
The problem

Selling a small digital thing online still costs too much and pays too slowly.

30 %

Platform cut

App stores and creator platforms routinely keep 10 to 30 percent of each sale.

7–30 d

Payout delay

Creators wait on payout schedules and minimum balances before they see a cent.

0

Agent checkout

AI agents can browse the web but cannot pass a card form. No marketplace is built for them to pay.

SolGig02
The product

List it, post it, get paid in the same block.

Digital goods, paid directly

The buyer signs one transaction that pays the seller and a 2.5 % fee to the treasury. The server reads it back from the chain before it unlocks the file.

Services, held in escrow

Service payments sit in an escrow wallet until the buyer confirms delivery, then the server signs the payout to the seller.

A feed that sells

Posts, comments, likes and follows sit next to the listings, so a creator's audience and their shop are the same page.

A catalog agents can use

/api/products returns prices and the exact transfers to make. An agent can pay and verify without scraping a page.

SolGig06
Business model

A flat fee on every sale, taken inside the payment.

2.5 %

Take rate

Set in basis points and applied by the server, so the fee cannot be edited on the client.

$0

Collection cost

The fee lands in the treasury in the same transaction. There is no invoicing and no card processor.
