HOW X402 WORKS HERE

Nothing ships until the chain agrees.

1 · GET

402 with a signed challenge, 5 min price lock

2 · PAY

One Solana tx: seller net plus 2.5% fee

3 · RETRY

X-PAYMENT: signature plus payer's signed proof

4 · 200

File plus receipt in X-PAYMENT-RESPONSE

Before step 4 the server reads the tx from RPC and checks signer, network, time and balance changes (lamports, or USDC by owner and mint). One signature settles one purchase; a reused one gets 409.

Code: src/lib/x402.ts, src/lib/solana/pay.ts · 11 tests in tests/x402.test.ts · 5

Step one returns a challenge signed by the server that fixes the price for five minutes. Step two is a normal Solana transaction. In step three the agent also signs the challenge and the signature with its key, so nobody who merely saw the transaction on-chain can claim the file. Only then does the server check the chain and answer 200.
