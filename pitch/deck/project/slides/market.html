Source: Colosseum Copilot project search (Cypherpunk, Breakout, Radar, Renaissance), 26 Sep 2026 · 6

We searched past Colosseum hackathons with Copilot. The prize winners in this space, MCPay, Corbits and Latinum, are rails that let agents pay for API calls. Freelance escrow marketplaces exist but serve people. Tedix is closest to us. No one we found runs a headless merchant catalog where many sellers list goods for agents. We have not checked Tedix beyond its public description, so the slide calls it the closest match rather than claiming a gap.
